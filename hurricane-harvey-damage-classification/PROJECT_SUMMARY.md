## Resume-ready summary

**Hurricane Harvey Damage Classification — Published Research**

Developed and compared deep-learning architectures for post-hurricane satellite-image damage classification using Python and TensorFlow/Keras, including custom CNN, VGG16-style and AlexNet-style architectures. Evaluated four architectures using accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and ROC/AUC analysis; the published paper reports approximately 93% accuracy for the highest-performing custom model.
